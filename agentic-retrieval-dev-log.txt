- `createExecutorState(repoRoot, limits)` holds lazy repo index (sorted source files), read counter, observation-byte budget.

- `executeReadFile` respects `start_line`/`end_line` window, caps lines at `maxLinesPerRead`, truncates at `maxBytesPerRead`, and charges `maxFileReads`.

- `executeSearchPaths` substring match against the lazy repo index (cap 25 matches).
- `executeFollowImports` resolves relative specifiers with `.ts`/`.tsx`/`.js`/`.mjs`/`.cjs` + `/index.*` candidates, marks unresolved ones.
- `executeActions(state, actions)` runs actions sequentially, produces a `RoundObservationList`, and short-circuits when the observation-byte budget would be exceeded.

**AR-P4-T3 — bounded loop + injection**: New `src/retriever/agent.ts`:
- `runRetrieverAgent({ repoRoot, intent, scout, model, limits })` drives the round loop.

- Final round forces `forceStop=true`. On parse/model error, falls through to `fallbackRecommendation` synthesized from the scout seed.
- `sanitizeRecommendation` normalizes all file paths to repo-relative and drops any that escape `repoRoot`.
- Telemetry: `roundsExecuted`, `actionsExecuted`, `fileReadsExecuted`, `observationBytes`, `stopReason` (`agent_stopped` | `round_cap` | `action_cap` | `model_error` | `parse_error`).
