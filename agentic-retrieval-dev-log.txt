**AR-P3-T1 — scout module + curated terms**: New `src/retriever/scout.ts` exports `buildScoutTerms` and `runScout`. Terms are deterministic, weighted by provenance (focus=4, tag=3, restatement=2, intent=1), deduped with origin aggregation, stop-word filtered, and capped at MAX_TERMS=24. Tokenization is shared by splitting camelCase + `[._/-]` separators. The scout is the only file-reading retrieval path in Phase 3 and is separated from the later agent loop.

**AR-P3-T2 — ranked candidates, tiers, role hints**: `runScout` discovers files deterministically (sorted walk), scores each with weighted term/symbol/path matching, and splits results into `selected` (<=8) and `reserve` (<=4) tiers. Each `ScoutCandidate` carries: stable rationale string, `ScoutFileRole` (`tagged` | `entry-point` | `config` | `doc` | `test` | `implementation` | `unknown`), `RetrievalDefaultEvidenceMode` hint (short tagged files → whole_file; strong symbol score → spans; docs/config → summary; otherwise summary/summary+ast), top symbol hints, imports, AST skeleton, and lineCount. Tagged files get a +50 boost and always win ranking ties.

**AR-P3-T3 — dispatch wiring + deterministic tests**: `src/services/retrieval-dispatch.ts` now runs `runScout` explicitly before the retriever worker and the prompt assembler (the scout result is injected into both so no re-scanning happens). `src/retriever/worker.ts` reduced to orchestration: `runRetrieverWorker` either uses an injected scout or runs one, then `scoutToRawOutput` converts candidates into `RawRetrievalOutput` with per-file `selection_tier`, `selection_reason`, `default_evidence_mode`, plus top-level `strategy_summary` and `scout_terms`. `src/retriever/normalize.ts` propagates those new raw fields (with validated coercion to the v1 union types) into the stored `retrieval-index-v1` artifact. `src/retriever/prompt.ts` now shares term generation with the scout via `buildScoutTerms` and accepts a `scoutTerms` pass-through.

- `tests/retriever/scout.test.ts` (new, 14 tests): weight ordering by origin, origin aggregation, stop-word exclusion, determinism, narrow bounded output, tagged-file role+boost, retrieval-focus steering, rationale/evidence-mode population, selected/reserve exclusivity, no-raw-body leak, strategy summary shape, cross-file hints, and a "narrower than the old 12-file heuristic default" guard on a 20-file repo.
